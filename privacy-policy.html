Up-To-Theta 100% Private

Last Updated: September 2026

Summary: Up-To-Theta is built with a local-first philosophy. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any personal data. All your tasks, timers, streaks, and settings remain 100% on your local computer.

1. Information We Collect

Up-To-Theta does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII). There are no user accounts, no tracking analytics, and no remote server databases.

2. How Local Data is Stored

The extension uses Chrome's built-in local storage API (chrome.storage.local) solely on your own browser to save:

  • Your custom timer preferences (focus duration, break duration, audio volume).
  • Your task list, due dates, reminder times, and task completion logs.
  • Your focus statistics, streaks, and heatmap data.
  • Your list of custom websites to block during focus sessions.

This data never leaves your device and is never shared with third parties or external servers.

3. Browser Permissions & Why They Are Used

  • Storage: Required to save your settings and task records locally on your device.
  • Alarms: Required to calculate timer countdowns and deliver task reminder alerts reliably even when the extension popup is closed.
  • Notifications: Required to alert you when a focus period finishes, a break starts, or a scheduled task reminder arrives.
  • Tabs: Required to open or switch focus to your Up-To-Theta dashboard tab when you click on notifications or the extension icon.
  • DeclarativeNetRequest & Host Permissions (<all_urls>): Exclusively used during active focus sessions to block user-specified distracting websites (e.g. social media) and redirect them to your local focus screen. No browsing history is tracked, logged, or sent anywhere.

4. Third-Party Services

Up-To-Theta does not use third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or external ad networks. All fonts, icons, and audio synthesis are bundled locally within the extension.

5. Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices change, this policy will be updated immediately. However, our commitment to keeping your focus data private on your local device will remain unchanged.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official extension support page.
