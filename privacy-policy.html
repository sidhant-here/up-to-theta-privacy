Up-To-Theta 100% Private

Last Updated: September 2026

Summary: Up-To-Theta was designed with a local-first approach in mind. None of your personal data is collected, transmitted, sold, or monetized. Everything you set up stays entirely locally on your computer.

1. Information We Collect

Up-To-Theta DOES NOT collect, store, or transfer any personally identifiable information (PII). No user accounts, tracking analytics, or remote databases exist.

2. How Local Data is Stored

The extension utilizes only Chrome's built-in local storage API (chrome.storage.local) on your own browser to store:

  • Your timer configuration (focus time, break time, audio volume level).
  • Your task list, deadlines, reminder times, and history of completed tasks.
  • Focus statistics, streaks, and heatmaps information.
  • Your custom websites that should be blocked while you're in the focus mode.

This data never leaves your device and never gets shared anywhere else.

3. BROWSER PERMISSIONS AND WHY THEY ARE USED

  • Storage: Needed to store your settings and tasks in the local storage of your computer.
  • Alarms: Needed to do the timer calculations and provide reminders about tasks regardless of whether the extension popup is open.
  • Notifications: Needed to notify you that your focused session is over, a break has begun, or there is a reminder about your task.
  • Tabs: Needed to launch or switch to your Up-To-Theta dashboard tab when you get notifications or press on the extension icon.
  • DeclarativeNetRequest & Host Permissions (<all_urls>): Used only while your session of focus for blocking distracting websites according to your choice (for example, social media) and redirecting them to your local focus screen. No tracking, logging, or sending of any browsing history takes place.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Up-To-Theta does not use third-party analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, and advertising networks. The fonts, icons, and voice synthesis are stored locally in the extension.

5. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

In case of changes in our privacy policy, we will update this page immediately. However, our policy regarding storing your focus data locally on your computer will stay the same.
